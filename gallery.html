Teacher's Day


Tech Club celebrated Teacher's Day on 05th September 2024 remembering the role of each of our teachers in our lives. Members of Tech Club gifted each teacher with a "Lucky" Plant hoping to bring happiness and luck towards them.

Orientation


Tech Club conducted a fun and engaging orientation for our new batch of FY's on 16th August 2024, welcoming them into the world of Technology and beyond through our Club. Informing them of the roles and responsibilities they could undertake in the constructive environment of our Club, while having fun in the process.

Fiestron'24


Fiestron'24 was our flagship event of the year. It was the annual Tech Fest we conducted on 29th and 30th January. The fest featured numerous events and games which brought students across colleges to compete in a healthy-competitive environment. The theme of our fest was Metaverse and we showcased it through our thought philosophy and our decorations.

CL Meet


Our CL MEET for Fiestron'24 was conducted on 23rd January 2024, which focused on showcasing rich talents from CL's all around the cities. It was a fun and exciting event where we unveiled the theme and brochure of Fiestron'24.
