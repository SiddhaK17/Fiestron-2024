Teacher's Day


Tech Club celebrated Teachers Day on 5th September 2024 remembering the role of each of our teachers in our lives. Members of Tech Club gifted each teacher with a "Lucky" Plant hoping to bring happiness and luck towards them.

Fiestron'24


Fiestron'24 was our flagship event of the year. It was the annual Tech Fest we conducted on 29th and 30th January. The fest featured numerous events and games which brought students across colleges to compete in a healthy-competitive environment. The theme of our fest was Metaverse and we showcased it through our thought philosophy and our decorations.
