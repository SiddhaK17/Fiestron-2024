Welcome to the Tech Club

Our Essence!


The Tech Club is a vibrant community within the Computer Science Department, dedicated to fostering a passion for technology among students. Established in 2014, we evolved from the annual tech fest "Pegasus," which began in 1999 and transformed into "Techrenagate." Our rich history reflects our commitment to developing technical skills in a creative and collaborative environment. Our diverse membership brings together unique perspectives, enhancing our community.

Our primary goal is to provide students with the tools and connections to succeed in the tech landscape. We encourage members to pursue innovative projects while building friendships and professional networks.

Our Purpose!


The Tech Club uniquely combines academic enrichment with hands-on activities. We organize workshops, hackathons, and tech talks to enhance learning experiences. Our specialized departments—Marketing, PR, Hospitality, Design, and Logistics—empower members to explore their interests and actively contribute to the club

We promote a culture of creativity and teamwork, encouraging all members to share ideas and collaborate on projects that inspire them. By joining the Tech Club, you will develop your skills, expand your network, and make meaningful contributions to both the club and the tech community.
