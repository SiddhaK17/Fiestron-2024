About Tech Club

Of K.C College

Who are we?

The Tech Club is an energetic and dynamic community within the Computer Science Department, dedicated to inspiring a deep appreciation for technology among students. Founded in 2014, our club evolved from an annual tech fest that started in 1999 as "Pegasus" and transitioned through several iterations to become "Techrenagate." This rich history illustrates our commitment to not only fostering technical skills but also cultivating an environment where creativity and collaboration thrive. Our membership encompasses a broad spectrum of individuals, each contributing unique perspectives and talents, creating a multifaceted community

Our core objective is to equip students with the tools and connections necessary to excel in the tech landscape. By offering a supportive atmosphere, we encourage members to embark on projects that push technological boundaries while forging friendships and professional networks. We invite everyone to engage with us and become part of a tradition of innovation and shared growth.

What we do?

The Tech Club distinguishes itself by seamlessly integrating academic enrichment with hands-on extracurricular activities. We host an array of workshops, hackathons, and tech talks that engage members and enhance their learning experiences. Each of our specialized departments—including Tech Support, Marketing, PR, Hospitality, Design, and Logistics—focuses on specific operational aspects, empowering members to take ownership of their interests and contribute meaningfully to the club's initiatives.

We emphasize a culture of creativity and teamwork, where all members can voice their ideas and collaborate on projects that resonate with their passions. By joining the Tech Club, you will not only hone your skills but also expand your professional network, making significant contributions to both the club and the broader tech community.
